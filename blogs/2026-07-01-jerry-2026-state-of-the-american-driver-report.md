For the fifth straight year, car ownership costs are climbing faster than drivers can absorb. The Jerry 2026 State of the American Driver report finds premiums cutting into grocery and retirement budgets at every income level, the drivers most willing to be tracked carrying the worst records, and a strong sentiment on hybrids versus EVs.
