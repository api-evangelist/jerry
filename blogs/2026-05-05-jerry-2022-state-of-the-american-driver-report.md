The 2022 State of the American Driver report offers insights into how the ongoing pandemic is reshaping car ownership for Americans, how they are reacting to chip shortages and rising gas prices, their outlook for buying and selling vehicles in the year ahead, their views on electric and autonomous vehicles, and their attitudes toward commuting, ride-hailing and the cost of driving.
