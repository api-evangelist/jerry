The 2023 State of the American Driver report offers insights into how a second pandemic-era year of supply shortages and the strongest inflation in four decades is affecting American car owners, the financial burden many take on to stay on the road, their growing interest in electric vehicles after a year of record-high gas prices, their commuting habits, and their views on rising road rage and driver safety.
